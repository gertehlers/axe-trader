Inside the spread

The signal is real. When RSI(7) drops below 30 and price closes at or below the lower Bollinger band, OIL_BRENT reverts — about ten times the base rate, in both samples. It just doesn't revert far enough to pay the spread.

Every chart below has a break-even line. That is the entry plus one round-trip spread. Price has to close beyond it for the trade to make a penny.

Watch how often the move is real, obvious, in the right direction — and stops short of that line. That is the whole finding, and it is the fourth time this project has met it.

What you are looking at. 400 signals drawn at random (seed 20260919) from the 17,900 that fired, so the losers are here in proportion. Not a curated set.

What would change the verdict. Ground where the round trip costs well under the 0.09–0.19 ATR this signal delivers. On the instruments we hold that means hourly bars or slower, where there are only 100–500 signals per sample — too few to measure. If you see something in these charts that says otherwise, mark it.

    Why this is not a strategy

    • The edge is smaller than the spread at every fast timeframe. 0.094 ATR against a 0.484 ATR round trip on discovery; 0.190 against 0.300 on validation. At best it breaks even, and that is before slippage, which is not modelled here at all.
    • No ground clears its cost in both samples. Across 24 instrument × timeframe × sample cells, every cell that cleared in one flipped sign in the other — OIL_CRUDE 4h went 2.39 to −2.87 on 220 then 108 observations. Those are small samples, not edges.
    • The first version of this analysis was nearly a mirage. Scoring indicators against ZigZag pivots showed a ×4.7 lift with 94% of runs going up. An up-leg starts at a swing low by definition, and a swing low is where RSI is low and price is at the lower band — it rediscovers the definition of a pivot. Forward returns are the honest measure and they are far smaller.
    • This is exploratory. The horizon was chosen as the best of four, 24 cells were examined, and nothing here was pre-registered. It cannot promote anything on its own.
    • But it does correct one earlier claim. Direction is not unavailable on oil — it is unavailable to continuation designs, which is every momentum hypothesis in the ledger. As mean reversion it exists.
