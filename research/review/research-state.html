Four hypotheses have been tested and none survived. That is the expected outcome and not the news. The news is why: three of them were aimed at the most expensive ground on the board, and until today nobody had measured which ground was which.

the common cause

H-0001 had no signal. H-0002 and H-0003 both found a real direction whose entire edge was eaten by the spread. All three were run on US500 5-minute bars — a choice nobody ever justified, inherited from the archived Java config.

the finding · spec §6.1 step 1

We were hunting on the 13th-cheapest of 19 grounds

For each instrument and timeframe: what share of a typical bar's range does one round trip consume? A strategy must capture more than this before it earns anything at all. Lower is better hunting.

H-0004 · known-effect source

The overnight premium is real. Financing takes 46% of it.

The most replicated equity anomaly says the whole index return accrues while the cash market is shut. This broker charges 1.45 points a night to hold it. That is a question with a number on both sides, and 665 cash sessions to answer it.

why it is parked, not banked

The +1.168 points per session that survives costs sits against a standard deviation of 36.6. Resolving an edge that small at 80% power needs 7,689 sessions — about 31 years. We have 664. Under the spec's diagnosis layers, layer 2 passes (costs do not eat the edge, they eat 46% of it) and layer 0 fails, so the status is inconclusive: park until more data exists. The parked condition is a longer history, not a better idea — and re-cutting the same 664 sessions by weekday or regime only makes the power worse.

H-0003 · layer-2 follow-up

Holding longer does not outrun the spread

Spread is paid once however long a trade is held, so a longer hold should earn more against the same fixed cost. The same 96-cell grid, re-run at each horizon with its own placebo.

what happens next

Aimed at 4h and daily, from a source that has never been mined

research/sources/known-effects.md lists five candidates, each recording its economic reason, its losing side, and the power we actually have. An effect with no identifiable loser is a pattern in a sample.

Brent–WTI spread mean reversion

The only candidate using two instruments we both hold at minute resolution, and close to market-neutral — so index drift cannot contaminate the reading, which is exactly what flattered H-0003. Killable fast: first measure whether daily spread changes even exceed the 0.0765-point two-leg cost, before building anything.

~1 hour to a verdict · OIL_CRUDE + OIL_BRENT · daily and 4h

EIA Wednesday reaction on OIL_CRUDE

A scheduled information release that forces position adjustment at a known moment. No external calendar needed for a first look — "Wednesday 15:30 UTC" is right most weeks, and the US-holiday shift weakens a measured effect rather than inventing one.

~135 Wednesdays · expect a layer-0 power problem · 4h

Turn-of-the-month, and opening-range breakout

Recorded, not scheduled. The turn-of-month effect has ~31 month-turns in our window — inconclusive before it starts. Opening-range breakout has the weakest losing side on the list and needs 15m or 5m bars, the most expensive ground on the board.

carry this forward

One result is statistically solid and it is a prohibition rather than an edge: holding US500 short overnight loses reliably — −3.774 points per session, confidence interval entirely below zero, positive in only 2 of 11 quarters. Bank it as a risk control for any future strategy on this instrument.
