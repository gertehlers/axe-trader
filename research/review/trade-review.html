Four exits, one entry

The confluence entry is frozen, so all four exit arms mostly trade the same setups and differ only in where they get out. That makes the exits comparable candle by candle — and it is the only honest way to decide which arms the 51-config matrix should spend its budget on.

Nothing here is profitable. The question is not which arm wins, but whether the 1:1 arm is broken for a structural reason — and the data says it is.

H-0005 measured this entry's edge by forward horizon and found it rising with time held: 50th percentile at 15m, 97.9 at 2h, 100.0 at 4h, 89.4 at 8h, on OIL_CRUDE SHORT. Every arm here exits long before that. Mean holds are 0.27h (reversal), 0.71h (symmetric), 1.50h (time), 2.17h (trailing) — all four cut inside the horizon band where the edge was not yet measurable.

Worse for the symmetric arm specifically: held to 4h the SHORT side averages +0.2604 ATR but its median is −0.4147 at a 42.9% win rate. The edge is tail-driven — a minority of large moves. A symmetric 1:1 target caps every winner at 1R, which is precisely where a tail-driven edge lives. It cannot capture this edge by construction, so its −0.218R is not bad luck to be averaged out over more instruments.

What would still be true if you believed all of this

  • Slippage is 0 in every figure here. Every cost number is a floor. The trailing arm exits 456 of 456 trades via STOP and the symmetric arm 294 of 482 — exactly where slippage bites hardest — so the wide-brake arms are flattered most by this.
  • This is one instrument. D3 asks whether an ATR-normalised exit behaves differently on US500 than on oil; that is unanswered until the matrix runs on more than OIL_CRUDE.
  • H-0005 is a lead, not a finding. 30 cells were examined, so a percentile of 100.0 does not clearly clear a Bonferroni bar; and OIL_BRENT showing the same shape is not independent evidence, because Brent and WTI are the same underlying.
  • The LONG side looks dead on this instrument — negative at the 4h horizon (−0.3694 ATR) and in all four arms. H-0005 put both oil LONG sides at percentiles 0–31. Enabling LONG in the matrix spends budget on a side already measured as having no edge.
  • Weekend financing still multiplies by 1.0. Holds here average 0.27–2.17h so carry is rare, but it stays wrong for any arm that holds toward the 4h horizon the edge actually needs.
